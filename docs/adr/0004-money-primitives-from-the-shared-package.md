# Money Primitives Come from the Shared `@trajectonomics/money` Package

Converting a major-unit amount to integer Minor Units and back is the one operation `@trajectonomics/tax` and `@trajectonomics/ledger` both perform at their boundaries, and each package used to carry its own copy. The copies were deliberate and unrecorded, and they had already diverged: the ledger's `toPrecision(15)` trim rounded near-ties the other way and lost digits above roughly 10¹³ major units, `-0` came back signed there and normalised here, and a negative amount threw here by default but converted there. A consumer that converts with one package and posts with the other was silently off by one Minor Unit, and neither suite could see it. #22 settled the deferral from #21 in favour of extraction.

We take `@trajectonomics/money` (`0.1.0`) as a runtime dependency and delegate both conversions to it. `toMinorUnits` and `fromMinorUnits` keep their signatures, defaults and documented behaviour, and they rebuild the shared package's `MoneyValidationError` as this package's `TaxValidationError` field for field, so the emitted `name`, message shape, `field`, `value` and `reason` are unchanged and no consumer can tell the arithmetic moved.

Precision stays local: `CURRENCY_PRECISION` remains the parameters-as-data source of the `digits` argument, so the shared package learns nothing about currencies and nothing here touches `Intl`. The branded `Money<C>` type and its constructors, the validators, `roundHalfUp`, `applyPercentage`, `applyGrossUp`, `clampCap` and `resolveCap` stay where they were. The shared surface is bounded to the two conversions and the validation they need, and it grows one function at a time and only when a second consumer exists, so it never becomes a rounding or statutory-cap framework.

The usage rule the shared package documents applies here unchanged: convert a major-unit amount once at the boundary, carry integer Minor Units through every stage, and pass `allowNegative` when a refundable credit can make the net figure negative. The canonical decision and that guidance live in `trajectonomics/money`; this ADR only records the adoption.
